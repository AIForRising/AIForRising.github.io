What we're building

Four directions, one thesis: AI that earns its place in the work.

Shipping

Applied intelligence

Models and systems built to land in real products — reliable, observable, and genuinely used, not just demoed.

Infra

Agent infrastructure

Memory, tooling, and runtime that let agents do long, real work without falling over halfway through.

Research

Evals & alignment

Measuring what models actually do, so progress is honest and safety is a property of the system, not a slogan.

Community

Open source

Everything we learn, released back — code and writeups, including the parts that didn't work.

Manifesto

What we hold to.

  1. 01

    Build for the people behind the work.

    Tools should lift the person using them — not replace the thinking that makes them good in the first place.

  2. 02

    Open beats closed.

    Research, code, and the hard-won lessons go back to the community. Progress compounds when it's shared.

  3. 03

    Measure before you claim.

    Progress only counts when we can show our work. We publish the eval, not just the headline.

  4. 04

    Dependability over spectacle.

    A model that ships and stays honest beats a demo that wows once and breaks on the second try.
